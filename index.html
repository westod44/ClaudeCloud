How to survive

  • Move: tap or click the ground, or use WASD. Tap a tree, rock, bush, animal or building to walk over and use it.
  • Stay alive: watch your health, food, water, stamina and energy. Drink at water or wells, eat berries and cooked meat.
  • Start: gather sticks and fiber from bushes and stones from rocks with your bare hands. Then craft Rope and a Stone axe (Pack → Crafting).
  • Build: chop trees for logs. Build a campfire, then a lean-to for sleeping and saving. Palisade walls and spike traps protect your base.
  • Night: the Hollows come out of the dark. They fear fire, so stay near your fires or carry a torch. Brutes appear from day 4.
  • Goal: find the 8 relics in the island's shrines, build the Signal Beacon, light it, and survive until dawn to be rescued.
  • Keys: E use · F/Space attack · Q switch weapon · T torch · I pack · B build · C centre · Esc menu · +/− zoom.

LANTERN ISLES

Your progress saves when you sleep, at each dawn, and from the menu.
