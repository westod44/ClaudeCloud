How to survive

  • Interact: click or tap anything: trees, rocks, bushes, animals, buildings, your own structures. You walk there and do it. If there are several options (a fire: cook, add a log, add a stick…) a menu appears.
  • Move: click the ground, or WASD / arrows. Shift sprints (uses stamina). Space dodges.
  • Fight: aim with the mouse and right-click (or F) to swing toward the cursor. Bows and slings fire where you aim. On touch screens, drag from the Attack button to aim and release to strike.
  • Stay alive: health, food, water, stamina and warmth. Cold and rain chill you, so stay near fires and shelters. Bandages stop bleeding, herbs and salves cure sickness.
  • Build: logs and stones build a base: walls that join up, gates, floors, a bed, a table, a drying rack, a cooking pot, a garden, storage, and a workbench for advanced gear.
  • Night: Hollows come out of the dark and fear fire. The outer reaches of the island are the most dangerous, and new creatures appear as the days pass.
  • Goal: find the 8 relics, build the Signal Beacon, light it at dusk and survive until dawn.
  • Pack & storage: click a stack to pick it up and click a slot to place it (or drag). Right-click takes half or drops one. Shift-click sends it across. Hover and press 1–6 to put it on the hotbar, E to use it, Q to drop it.
  • Keys: E use · F/right-click attack · Space dodge · Shift sprint · Q switch weapon · 1–6 hotbar · T torch · I pack · B build · C centre · Esc menu.
